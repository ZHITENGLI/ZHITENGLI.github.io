Zhiteng Li

I am a third-year master student at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Yulun Zhang and Prof. Linghe Kong. Previously, I received my B.E. degree from SJTU in 2023.

My research interests lie in LLM pretraining and scaling, image/video generation, model compression and inference acceleration (e.g., Quantization), and data synthesis.

I interned at Amazon ASAIL, Sony AI, Xiaohongshu, and Bytedance Seed-LLM.
